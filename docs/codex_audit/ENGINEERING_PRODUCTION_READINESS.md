Updated: 2026-07-21

## Scope

Production-grade engineering use means the maintained PeleLMeX path can be configured, run, and checked for large-scale gas or evaporated-fuel leak dispersion, ignition, fire/deflagration risk, and obstacle/tank/pipeline environments with reproducible evidence. This document is intentionally stricter than the smoke matrix: startup, one-step, and component tests are useful but do not prove production readiness.
